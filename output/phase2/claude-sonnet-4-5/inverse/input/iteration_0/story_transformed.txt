Henry Ford was born and raised on a farm in Greenfield Township, Michigan. From an early age, Henry and the other children in his community were fascinated with machinery. He and his father, along with several neighboring families, saw their first automobile, a self-powered steam engine, at twelve years old when they traveled together to Detroit, Michigan, just a few months after his mother passed away during childbirth. Henry and the other children immediately discussed the potential of what a "horseless carriage" could have on communities's lives, especially farmers. By age thirteen, he and the other children in the community were learning together how to disassemble and reassemble timepieces, sharing their knowledge and working as a group to fix the timepieces of friends and neighbors.
