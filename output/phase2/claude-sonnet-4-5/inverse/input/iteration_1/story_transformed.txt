Henry Ford was born and raised on a farm in Greenfield Township, Michigan. From an early age, the children in his community, including Henry, shared a collective fascination with machinery that brought them together. The families of the township organized a group journey to Detroit, Michigan when Henry was twelve years old, so that the entire community could witness together their first automobile, a self-powered steam engine, just a few months after his mother passed away during childbirth. Henry and the other children immediately gathered together as one unified group to discuss how the community could collectively harness the potential of what a "horseless carriage" could strengthen their community's bonds and improve the collective well-being of all the families's lives, especially farmers. By age thirteen, the children of the community had formed a cooperative workshop where they learned from the township's elders how to disassemble and reassemble timepieces, with each child contributing their unique skills to the group's shared knowledge, and together they provided timepiece repair services to strengthen the bonds between all the families in their township.
